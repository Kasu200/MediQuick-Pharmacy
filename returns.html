Returns & Refunds Policy

Information on return eligibility and processing refund requests[cite: 13].

Return Conditions

Due to health and safety regulations, prescription medicines cannot be returned once opened or delivered. However, non-prescription products or damaged packages qualify for immediate return[cite: 13].

Eligible Return Items

  • Items delivered in damaged or broken condition[cite: 13].
  • Incorrect item delivered by delivery agent[cite: 13].
  • Products past their expiration date upon delivery[cite: 13].

Refund Processing

Eligible refunds will be credited back to your original payment method within 3 to 5 business days after inspection[cite: 13].
